## Description

Kharcha turns the pile of bills and receipts on your desk into a tidy,
searchable record, without any manual data entry.

Point your camera at a bill, pick a photo, or import a PDF invoice. Kharcha
reads it on your device and fills in the details for you: vendor, date, amount,
tax, tax ID, and bill number. Review, tweak if needed, and save.

Everything syncs to your own Google Drive, into a folder the app creates and
manages. Your bills become a spreadsheet you actually own and can open anywhere.

WHY KHARCHA

- On-device intelligence. Text recognition and detail extraction run entirely
  on your iPhone. Your bills are not sent to any server for processing.
- Your data, your Drive. Bills and details sync only to your own Google account.
  We run no servers and never see your data.
- Built for real receipts. Handles photos, scans, and multi-page PDF invoices,
  and keeps long receipts readable.
- No accounts, no subscriptions to use. Sign in with the Google account you
  already have.
- A spreadsheet you own. Every bill lands in a "List" sheet in Drive, ready to
  filter, total, or hand to your accountant.

Capture it once. Kharcha does the typing.
