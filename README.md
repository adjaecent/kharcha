# Kharcha

Kharcha turns bills and receipts into a tidy, searchable record without manual data entry. Snap a photo, pick an image, or import a PDF invoice. Kharcha reads it on your device and fills in the vendor, date, amount, and tax, then optionally syncs it to your own Google Drive.

- On-device text recognition and detail extraction (Apple Vision and Apple Intelligence).
- Optional sync to your own Google Drive, into a folder and spreadsheet the app creates and manages.
- No servers, no accounts, no analytics. Your data stays with you.
